## Description

Yohaku is a small app for leaving time with nothing planned—not for adding more to your schedule.

Give a space a name and time: “Do nothing,” “Have tea slowly,” or anything that feels like room to breathe. Then look back across quiet Today, Week, and Month views.

Features

• Create spaces with a name, date, start time, and end time
• Review a day in Today
• See a whole week at a glance
• Look back on marked days in Month
• Optional local notifications shortly before each space
• A quiet, optional one-time check-in after a space ends
• No timer or special mode interrupting the space itself
• System, light, and dark appearance
• No account; spaces stay on your device

Every core feature is free. The free version may show an unobtrusive banner ad. A one-time in-app purchase permanently removes ads. There is no subscription.
