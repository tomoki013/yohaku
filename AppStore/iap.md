# 非消耗型IAP

- Type: `Non-Consumable`
- Reference Name: `Remove ads permanently`
- Product ID: `io.tmkch.yohaku.removeads`
- Price: 日本で約300円になる価格帯（App Store Connect上の最新価格表から選択）
- Family Sharing: Off

## 日本語

- Display Name: `広告を永久に削除`
- Description: `一度の購入で広告を永久に削除し、Yohakuの開発を応援できます。`

## English (U.S.)

- Display Name: `Remove ads permanently`
- Description: `Permanently remove ads with a one-time purchase and support Yohaku.`

## Review Notes

Open the app, tap the gear icon in the top-right corner, and use the purchase card at the top of Settings. The price is loaded from StoreKit. This non-consumable purchase permanently removes banner ads. “Restore Purchase” is shown directly below the purchase button.
